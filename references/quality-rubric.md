# Headline Quality Rubric

Version: 0.1.0

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 using the anchors below. The default aggregate is
the weighted mean shown in the table. Scores are evidence for discussion, not a
substitute for editorial judgment.

| Dimension | Weight | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy and promise fit | 25 | false or unsupported | broadly true but loose | exactly earned by the work |
| Audience and purpose fit | 15 | wrong reader or action | usable but generic | clearly serves the intended reader/action |
| Genre and medium fit | 10 | violates genre or placement | workable with conditions | natural for the form and verified medium |
| Clarity and scanability | 15 | confusing or syntactically broken | understandable after effort | immediately legible without flattening meaning |
| Specificity and reader value | 15 | empty abstraction | some signal | gives a concrete reason to care |
| Voice fidelity | 10 | sounds like a different author | mixed or uncertain | preserves diction, rhythm, stance, and register |
| Distinctiveness | 5 | interchangeable | somewhat familiar | memorable because of earned material |
| Energy and memorability | 5 | inert or awkward | adequate | creates useful momentum without hype |

## Scoring anchors

- 0: disqualifying absence, contradiction, or damage.
- 1: materially weak; major revision required.
- 2: serviceable but generic, conditional, or uneven.
- 3: strong; minor refinement may help.
- 4: excellent for this brief; do not change it merely to chase novelty.

Compute the default weighted score as the sum of score divided by 4 multiplied by the
dimension weight. Preserve the eight raw scores so a reviewer can see what drove the
result.

## Critical gates

A candidate fails release when any of these is true:

- accuracy or promise fit is below 3;
- audience and purpose fit is below 3;
- genre or medium fit is below 3 when the medium is known;
- voice fidelity is below 3 when voice evidence is supplied;
- the line states unsupported fact, causation, scale, certainty, or urgency;
- the line uses a deceptive information gap or a promise the work does not deliver;
- the line replaces a protected term or qualifier without authorization.

A critical failure is not offset by a high score for cleverness, brevity, or energy.

## Tie-breaking

When two options have the same aggregate score:

1. prefer the more accurate promise;
2. prefer the clearer reader value;
3. prefer the more voice-faithful line;
4. prefer the less generic line;
5. prefer the shorter line only if medium fit and meaning remain equal.

## Evidence note

Every score of 3 or 4 should have a short evidence note. For a 0, 1, or critical
failure, record the repair or rejection reason. Do not use "sounds good" as the only
evidence.
